TerraRae 1. Overview TerraRae is a strategic board game for iPhone and iPad. One human player competes against 1–3 AI opponents on a **real world map**, racing to control the global supply of rare earth minerals. This policy applies to the version distributed via the App Store and TestFlight on iPad. 2. Quick summary * TerraRae does not operate its own server. All data is stored locally on your device. * TerraRae does not collect user data. It is a game. * TerraRae requires no sign-up. 3. Data the app handles 3.1. Data you enter into the app * No data is required. 3.2. Data generated by the app * Numbers computed to get to scores of the player against the system. 4. Analytics, advertising, and tracking * TerraRae does not display ads. * TerraRae does not use third-party analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, etc.). * TerraRae does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because it performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking. * Install, session, and crash metrics may be made available by Apple to the developer through App Store Connect and TestFlight, in aggregated form and/or per Apple's policies. This collection is performed by iPadOS, not by TerraRae. 5. Children TerraRae is intended for users at ages that understand the mechanism of the game. The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. 6. Security No personal data is stored in the app or cloud server. 7. iCloud backups If iCloud Backup is enabled on the device, the app's data may be included in the device's automatic backup as configured in iOS. That backup is managed by Apple, per its policies. 8. Your rights Because no personal data is local on your device, you have direct control over it: * Choices of avatar ad difficulty level within the app. 9. Changes to this policy We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when there are material changes. For significant changes, we will make a reasonable effort to communicate them within the app. 10. Contact Questions, requests, or reports related to this policy: E-mail: els19br@gmail.com
